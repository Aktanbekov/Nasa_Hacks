# Launch Window: 30-second video narration

Video: `demo/launch-window-demo.mp4` (29.8 s, 1920×1080).
Space Apps rule: the video pitch must be **no longer than 30 seconds**, hosted at a public link (YouTube or Google Drive).
Source: [Space Apps project submission guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/).

Facts used (NIFC WFIGS incident records, the same data the game replays): the Dixie Fire was discovered on **July 13, 2021** (local time) in Northern California and burned **963,309 acres**.

Delivery: quiet and slow for the first three lines, lift the energy at "This is Launch Window", finish confident.

## Plain text

July 13th, 2021. A small fire starts in a Northern California canyon. Before it's over, the Dixie Fire burns nearly a million acres. What if you'd built the satellite that was watching? This is Launch Window. Design it as a team. Every part costs mass, power and money. Do the real math. Launch. Then real California wildfires test what you built. Play solo, or with a crew of four. ## ElevenLabs tips

- **Voice:** a calm, warm documentary narrator.
- **Settings:** Stability ~40–50%, Similarity ~75%, Style 20–30%, Speaker boost on, speed 1.0.
- **Break tags** work on Multilingual v2, Turbo and Flash. For **Eleven v3**, remove the tags and use line breaks and ellipses instead.
- **Length:** the audio must end before 29.8 s. If it runs long, raise speed to 1.05–1.1 or shorten the breaks.
- **Pronunciation:** read "2021" as "twenty twenty-one".
- Export **MP3 or WAV, 44.1 or 48 kHz**.
